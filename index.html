Hunter Transmission Project — what it means for local businesses

The Hunter Transmission Project will connect the Central-West Orana and New England renewable zones to Newcastle by 2028 — bringing cheaper, cleaner energy to the 80% of NSW demand concentrated in the Hunter, Sydney and Illawarra corridor. Local businesses that lock in the right tariff structure now will be best placed to benefit.

Source: EnergyCo NSW, Hunter Transmission Project plan (Nov 2023)
